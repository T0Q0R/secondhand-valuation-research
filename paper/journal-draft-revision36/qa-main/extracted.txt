Abstract
We study budgeted verification when sellers can withhold recorded vehicle
attributes. Public absence does not identify concealment, and one seller’s
reportcandisplaceanother’squery.SAV-Valcombinesmissingness-awarepredic-
tion, suppression-value acquisition and nested policy-aware training. CDM-Val
optimizes a distribution over complete verification rules against finite, batch-
reallocated unilateral payoff rows. Queries use publicly absent fields, and failed
recorded-value replay consumes capacity. In the complete three-source, three-
seed main study at 10% capacity, SAV-Val has 11.42% less positive log-quote
inflationthantheordinary-benefitstaticrobustreferenceonMUCars,butmore
inflation on synthetic and Jordanian cohorts. Its feedback component compar-
isons are also heterogeneous. The complete fixed-estimator CDM study does
not establish a consistent advantage over a simple risk rule. Complete equal-
allowance retraining at the largest cap yields 20.41% less inflation on MUCars
but 30.09% and 27.09% more on synthetic and Jordanian cohorts against
ordinary-benefit pricing. Comparator adaptations, common-report transfer and
missingnessdiagnosticsfurtherrestrictgeneralimprovementclaims.Honestand
attackedvaluationerrorremainnecessaryalongsideincentivemeasures.Theevi-
denceconcernshistoricaladvertisedpricesandstipulatedresponses,ratherthan
transactions, observed concealment or physical inspection.

1 Introduction
Used goods are valued from incomplete descriptions of condition, use and configura-
tion.Qualityuncertaintyandvoluntarydisclosurehavealongeconomichistory[1–3].
A predictive price quote introduces a concrete incentive: omitting an unfavorable
recorded attribute can increase the quote. An accurate predictor on ordinary listings
therefore need not remain accurate after sellers choose which information to disclose.
The information available to the platform matters. Two possible source states of the
samelistingcanproduceidenticalmissing-mileagereports:inonethesellerwithhelda
recordedvalue,whileintheotherthesourceneverrecordedthatvalue.Theidentifier,
other public inputs and peer reports are held fixed. Figure 1 shows this boundary. A
deployment rule cannot distinguish these cases before a query. Recorded-value replay
mayrecoveravalueinthefirstcaseandfailinthesecond;bothattemptsconsumethe
shared verification capacity. This replay is a controlled computational intervention,
not physical inspection.
Strategicallymissingdataarealreadystudiedinregressionandclassification[4,5].
Costlyandactivefeatureacquisitionalsohaveestablishedmethods[6–10].Theempir-
icalquestionhereiswhetherlearningtosuppressconcealment-inducedquoteincreases
improvesonordinaryerror-reductionacquisitionandasimplerrobustpredictorwhen
listings compete for a fixed batch capacity. Changing one report can change which
other listings receive queries, so a single-listing information score alone does not
describe the deployed interaction.
Strategic classification and causal perspectives model responses to prediction
rules [11–13]; performative prediction addresses deployment-induced distribution
changes [14]. Recent work also jointly designs prediction, audits and penalties [15].
Their different actions and outcome models motivate response analysis but do not
establishanincentivepropertyforcontinuousquoteswithrecorded-valuereplay.Bud-
geted randomized audits of strategic reports already minimized incentives to lie in
threshold and top-k allocations [16]. Partial verification without payments also has
established incentive characterizations [17]. Estornell et al. check reported types and
removean allocation when a lie is detected, with an optional fine. Our query recovers
one recorded field, can fail while spending capacity, and changes a continuous quote
withoutanaddedpenalty.Ceppietal.’struthfulnesscharacterizationdiffersfromour
finite bound on stored deviation rows. These model distinctions frame the empirical
comparison; they are not new general audit guarantees.
Missing-data mechanisms and predictive treatment of missingness are also estab-
lished [18–20]. Our indicators cannot identify why a field is blank. Strong categorical
tree models [21] and comparisons with neural tabular models [22] motivate retain-
ing a tree control. Acquisition outputs share the same multi-output ExtraTrees
machinery[23],soanoutput-specificgainmustbeassessedagainstthatsharedimple-
mentation. Three diﬀiculties remain under this information boundary: natural and
strategic absence are observationally identical, a fitted feedback loop can reuse out-
comesindirectly,andcapacitycompetitioncouplesreportswithinabatch.Prediction
accuracy and maximum unilateral gain also require different evaluation endpoints.

3 Discussion
The study shows why an improvement over a basic masked predictor is an insuﬀi-
cienttestofstrategicvaluation.Staticrobustpricingandordinaryacquisitionremain
strong controls. Feedback, acquisition targets and mixture calibration have effects
that depend on the source and capacity. Differences in approximate responses, fitting
samples and target distributions are possible explanations, not causes established by
these experiments.
Inflationisalsoanincompleteobjective.Arulecanmakewithholdingunprofitable
by raising honest quotes. The generic envelope control makes this tradeoff explicit:
a zero inflation value need not imply accurate valuation. Honest and attacked MAE,
signed bias, failed queries and remaining deviation regret must accompany strategic
measures.Mixturecalibrationaddressesamaximumfinitedeviationcriterion,whereas
the original primary outcome is an average positive quote gap; neither should be
silently substituted for the other after observing results.
The sources contain asking prices rather than realized transaction values. They
do not contain observed concealment labels, measured seller utilities or physical
6

inspection outcomes. Recovered recorded values may themselves be inaccurate. The
stipulated attacker can withhold recorded optional groups, but cannot fabricate val-
ues, change identifiers, choose peers or collude. Retrospective capped cohorts and ten
evaluation batches per source limit representativeness. Intervals average paired seed
contrasts before resampling the shared batches and condition on the fitted models;
theydonotquantifyuncertaintyovernewtrainingpopulationsorrealsellerbehavior.
SAV targets cover the complete outer feedback loop, but teacher and deployment
sample sizes and selected weights can differ. CDM’s first source study uses disjoint
estimator, head and probability-calibration rows rather than an outer replication of
the complete mechanism. Its fixed twelve-rule bank and five calibration peer-profile
types are finite restrictions. Prospective independent markets, measured query costs
andobservedresponsesareneededbeforedeployment.Thecompletemainstudysup-
portssource-dependentoutcomes;thegeneralempiricalincrementofeithercandidate
remains unestablished.
