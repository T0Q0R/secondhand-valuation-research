\section{Introduction}
Information about condition, usage and configuration is central to the
valuation of used goods. Quality uncertainty in such markets has a long
economic history~\cite{akerlof1970lemons}, as do models of voluntary
disclosure~\cite{grossman1981disclosure,milgrom1981news}. A prediction system
adds a concrete disclosure incentive: a seller may omit a recorded attribute
if doing so raises its estimated price. Training an accurate predictor on
ordinary listings does not by itself establish that its quotes remain
accurate after sellers respond to its deployment.

Consider two alternative source states for the same vehicle listing,
holding its identifier, other public inputs and peer reports fixed.
In both, reported mileage is absent. In the first state the seller has
withheld a recorded mileage of 140,000 km; in the second the source never
recorded mileage. The number is illustrative. Before a query, a
platform cannot distinguish these cases from the report alone. A rule that
uses a simulation's concealment flag to select queries would therefore
receive information unavailable in deployment. After a query, a replay
may recover the recorded value in the first case and fail in the second.
Both attempts consume capacity. Figure~\ref{fig:missingness} makes this
information boundary explicit.

Strategic missingness is already studied in regression and
classification~\cite{zhang2020strategic,krishnaswamy2021withheld}. Active
feature acquisition also provides established methods for selecting
information subject to acquisition costs~\cite{janisch2019costly,
shim2018joint,li2021gsm,valancius2024aco}.
Budgeted randomized audits of strategic reports already minimized incentives
to lie in threshold and top-$k$ allocations~\cite{estornell2021audits}.
Partial verification without payments also has established incentive
characterizations~\cite{ceppi2019verification}.
In Estornell et al.'s model, an audit checks an agent's reported type;
a detected lie removes its allocation and can incur a fine. Here a query
attempts to recover one missing recorded field, can fail while consuming
capacity, and changes a continuous quotation without an added penalty.
Ceppi et al. characterize verification sufficient for truthful allocation
without transfers. Our finite rule-bank calibration instead bounds only
stored unilateral payoff rows; it supplies no general truthfulness
characterization. The differences define the experimental model, not a
proof that auditing or its optimization is new.
These literatures leave an
empirical question for the present setting: under a shared batch capacity,
does learning to suppress concealment-induced quote increases improve on
ordinary error-reduction acquisition and a simpler robust predictor?
Capacity makes this question consequential because a change in one
seller's report can alter another seller's chance of receiving a query.

We implement SAV-Val, a mask-aware predictor with a learned query score
and policy-aware residual training. The query target measures a reduction
in the positive quote gap relative to the same listing's honest report.
The honest reference and replay outcomes are used to construct targets
offline; runtime scoring receives only the public report, current quote
and candidate field. Feedback training uses post-query reports produced
by simulated responses to the actual allocation rule. Three outer folds
generate targets without allowing a held-out outcome into the
corresponding fitting and feedback loop.

We also implement counterfactual deviation-constrained mixture verification
(CDM-Val). It fixes the estimator and optimizes a distribution over complete
capacity-feasible verification rules using signed, batch-reallocated
unilateral payoff rows. Reports are locked before one private rule is drawn.
Its exact finite-set calibration guarantee is separated from held-out
performance; the fixed-estimator study does not establish an advantage over a
simple risk rule. Randomization and response-oracle optimization themselves
are established methods~\cite{jain2011doubleoracle}, not novelty claims here.

The contribution being investigated is this specific combination and its
measured value, rather than the invention of strategic learning, auditing,
or acquisition. We provide a reproducible implementation, an explicit
information boundary, independently audited hard capacities and unilateral
deviation checks, and matched controls for the action target and feedback
components. We distinguish a conditional optimization statement about an
additive score from a guarantee about actual price error or seller behavior.

This manuscript is a development draft. The complete three-source,
three-seed main study favors the full model over a simpler no-feedback
control on MUCars and favors that control on synthetic and Jordanian
records. Direct-comparator audits, all nine source/size retraining cohorts
and the ordinary-benefit allowance extension are complete and independently
audited; their outcomes remain source-dependent.
Accordingly, the present evidence
does not support a general performance advantage or disclosure-incentive
claim. The target is recorded advertised price, and seller responses are
stipulated simulations; neither actual transactions nor observed fraud
are inferred from these data.

\section{Related Work}
\subsection{Strategic disclosure and prediction}
Strategic classification studies prediction rules when people respond to
them~\cite{hardt2016strategic,dong2018preferences}; causal perspectives
separate changes to observed features from changes to underlying
outcomes~\cite{miller2020causal}. Performative prediction studies
deployment-induced distribution changes~\cite{perdomo2020performative}.
These works motivate modeling response, but their different action and
outcome assumptions do not automatically establish a property of our
withholding-and-replay game.

Zhang et al.~\cite{zhang2020strategic} address strategically missing data
in regression through support-margin imputation (SMI) using full and
reduced linear support-vector models. This is a direct regression
antecedent. The published formulation and variable-domain assumptions
must be preserved in any comparison; an ordinary SVR with arbitrary
imputation is not SMI. Krishnaswamy et al.~\cite{krishnaswamy2021withheld}
study withheld data for classification. Applying a classification
implementation to continuous price prediction would require an explicitly
identified adaptation. A recent joint prediction-and-audit
preprint~\cite{ebrahimi2026audit} further establishes auditing as an
existing strategic-design mechanism. Its linear acceptance setting,
feature changes and penalties differ from our continuous quotes and
recorded-value replay; a setting difference alone is not evidence of a
new learning contribution.

\subsection{Missingness-aware prediction}
Statistical missing-data mechanisms are established
concepts~\cite{rubin1976missing}. Prediction with missing data also has
theoretical and architectural treatments~\cite{lemorvan2021imputation,
lemorvan2020neumiss}. Our use of missing indicators and imputation is a
practical representation choice. It does not identify whether a particular
blank is naturally missing or strategically withheld. CatBoost provides
the categorical tabular backbone~\cite{prokhorenkova2018catboost}; work
revisiting deep tabular models~\cite{gorishniy2021tabular} motivates
retaining a strong tree-based control when assessing a neural residual.

\subsection{Acquisition and robustness}
Costly-feature classification~\cite{janisch2019costly}, joint feature
selection and prediction~\cite{shim2018joint}, generative acquisition
surrogates~\cite{li2021gsm}, dynamic information
selection~\cite{covert2023information}, and acquisition-conditioned
oracles~\cite{valancius2024aco} are acquisition antecedents. Our ordinary
benefit head is a local matched control, not an official reproduction of
any of these systems. The strategic suppression output differs in its
target, while both outputs share the same fitted multi-output
ExtraTrees head~\cite{geurts2006extratrees}. This makes it possible to
isolate the selected score without crediting shared machinery to one
variant alone.

Robust training also requires reporting where the benefit occurs and
whether ordinary prediction is harmed. Group-robust work emphasizes
that aggregate scores can obscure heterogeneous
errors~\cite{sagawa2019groups}. Our completed technical subgroup analysis
uses source missingness patterns, rather than protected-demographic
labels, and cannot be read as a demographic fairness assessment. More
generally, reduced strategic quote inflation is an incomplete objective:
a model can make withholding unprofitable by issuing consistently high
honest quotes. Honest error and signed quote bias must therefore remain
visible alongside the strategic measure.
