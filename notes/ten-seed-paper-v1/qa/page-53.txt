Recomputed cap responses and multiobjective trade-offs
Takeaway. The cap’s inflation reductions survive the saved response heuristic in this study, but
attacked error and failed-query rates can worsen. This is an empirical result, not an extension of
the fixed-peer theorem.
We recomputed reports for every source/seed/estimator/capacity/rule cell, using the original
four starts and up to 20 sweeps, frozen public scores and unchanged mixture weights. The uncapped
baseline uses the separately corroborated original responses. All 432 new cells and 432,000 listing
outcomes were replayed by an separate full-sort auditor. It checked 5,300 supported complete-
rule batch allocations and 5,570,112 legal rule–deviation outcomes. The auditor reconstructs final
quotations, currency absolute errors, query failures and remaining unilateral regret; it does not
