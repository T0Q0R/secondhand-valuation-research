Takeaway. Increasing the fitting-seed count preserves the negative synthetic result against the
original static robust comparator. This section uses ten seeds; preceding cap experiments retain
three.
We combine original seeds 13, 42 and 2026 with predetermined seeds 101, 211, 307, 401, 503, 601
and 701 under the same original protocol. All ten synthetic checkpoints pass the source-binding,
split, model-receipt and recorded-outcome checks: 620 method/capacity cells and 620,000 listing
outcomes. These outcome rows are repeated predictions, not independent evaluation examples.
The design contains 1,000 unique evaluation listings in ten shared batches of 100, with ten fitting-
seed realizations on fixed training records. JUCars and MUCars are reported separately below; the
completed union covers all thirty source/seed pairs.
At 10% capacity, SAV mean positive log-quote inflation is 0.021175 versus 0.012084 for static
robust without feedback, a 75.23% increase. Table 6 retains all five original contrasts. The benefit-
head variant is the original feedback-trained variant; it is not the separately tuned ordinary-benefit
benchmark. This extension does not isolate estimator, acquisition and feedback factors, and it
must not be presented as the matched factorial experiment.
For each contrast we construct the ten-seed by ten-batch paired difference matrix. We indepen-
dently resample seed indices and shared batch indices, retaining their crossed grid and within-cell
method pairing. These intervals capture empirical fitting-seed and evaluation-batch variation con-
ditional on the training records; they do not capture training-population uncertainty. Only ten
batch clusters are available, so nominal coverage is not established.
