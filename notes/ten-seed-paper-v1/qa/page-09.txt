Proposition 3 (Finite recovery-graph implication). On a fixed finite collection of record graphs,
if Lpath = 0, positive gain cannot increase along any path composed of their legal recovery edges.
Proof. Every summand in (3) is nonnegative. A zero finite sum makes each edge increment non-
positive; chaining these inequalities proves the claim.
This implication allows positive initial concealment gain. It provides no guarantee on unseen

anchor between steps despite its stopped gradient. Recovery-path regularization (RPL) is there-
