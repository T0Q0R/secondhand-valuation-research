7.2
Recovery-path regularization: improvement and costs
Takeaway. RPL lowers primary mean inflation across all thirty source–seed comparisons at the
declared unit-weight endpoint. Predictive, query-failure and convergence costs keep this from being
a dominance claim.
The ten-seed extension compared path weights 0, 0.1, 0.5, 1 at gap weight β = 0.5, plus path
weights 0, 0.5 at β = 0, over query capacities 0, 0.1, 0.3. All branches used no policy feedback and
the same two-round reset schedule. The original 162 cells were reused and 378 added, for 540
cells. Each positive path-weight branch is compared with its own same-gap zero-path control. RPL
changes the predictor; it is not a fixed-predictor acquisition comparison.
The original grid, including λ = 1, was committed before formal evaluation in 6643cbe. This is
a timestamped development grid, not independent preregistration or proof that the endpoint was
prespecified as the headline. Weight 0.5 retained the existing gap term’s numerical scale; neither
0.5 nor 1 is claimed optimal.
Inflation improvement and uncertainty.
At 10% capacity and λ = 1, β = 0.5, ratios of mean
inflation to the matched λ = 0 control fell by 50.41%, 26.58% and 23.81% on synthetic, JUCars and
MUCars. All ten fitting seeds in each source had lower inflation. The absolute paired reductions
were 0.006268, 0.003306 and 0.001573 log units. The exploratory simultaneous bands over 216 met-
ric contrasts were [0.004644, 0.007891], [0.001209, 0.005403] and [0.000624, 0.002522], respectively
(Figure 1). These conditional bands support the within-design direction without certifying finite-
sample coverage or new-population generalization. Smaller weights were less uniform: at λ = 0.1,
only six of ten seeds improved on each real source; at λ = 0.5, nine of ten did so. Appendix I
retains every seed.
Predictive and verification costs.
Table 4 reports absolute outcomes. At the unit endpoint,
honest MAE changed by +0.82%, −0.10% and −0.80%, while attacked MAE changed by −0.91%,
−0.02% and −0.87%. Failed attempts as a fraction of all listings changed by +1.10, +0.44 and
−0.12 percentage points. The synthetic honest error and failed-query increases also excluded zero
in the exploratory simultaneous bands; real-source MAE changes did not. Figure 2 shows all paired
seed changes, including unfavorable cases. Its means of seed ratios are distinct from the ratios of
means reported above. Inflation improvement alone therefore does not determine a deployment
choice.
Budget and loss-component boundaries.
Figure 3 includes zero queries, so estimator changes
remain separate from gains due to verification. Greater capacity can lower inflation while increas-
ing charged failed queries.
The matched path/gap comparison (Figure 4) shows that the path
intervention is not an alias for the existing gap term at the tested weights. It complements the
original eight-configuration, three-switch component ablation rather than turning its switches into
three independent innovations. The original three-seed recovery-edge diagnostic also showed lower
positive gain increments, which remained strictly positive; those measurements are retained as a
pilot in Appendix I and are not relabeled as ten-seed evidence.
