All 48 capped/full real-source feedback decompositions are retained. Capped components use ten

Thirty-seed projections divide seed and interaction terms by three; the batch-noise-free projec-

evidence or population coverage.
G.2
Concealment identification from saved utility summaries
For one listing let Ci = ciki be concealment cost, Gi positive expected quote inflation, Ni the
positive best-legal-utility gain over truth, and ρi remaining unilateral regret. Since truthful utility
is its honest quote,
Ci −ρi ≤Gi −Ni ≤Ci.
Thus max(G −N, 0) bounds mean cost below; adding maxi ρi and capping by 2c bounds it above.
Uniform-cost hidden-field bounds divide by c; heterogeneous costs in [.01, .03) give bounds using
.03 and .01, respectively. Dividing by mean heterogeneous cost would not identify mean hidden
fields. Positive-cost states were not saved; all 20 nonconverged batches remain in the bounds. The
inequality passes 768 exhaustive toy utility/returned-state checks. The paired zero-cost concealment
counts use saved states directly. Neither co-occurrence nor this accounting identity identifies a
causal mediation effect.
G.3
Restricted final-record honest-error pilot
Three frozen robust/no-feedback models per real source (seeds 13, 42 and 2026) enumerate comple-
tions of transmission, fuel and body condition from their training-encoder vocabularies, including
missing tokens. Mileage must be observed and fixed; other covariates stay fixed. Unsupported ob-
served categories and missing mileage abstain. The same original 1,000 records per source are used
without tuning or retraining. Current records are included in the minima; singleton sets preserve
their original quotes exactly. Shared-support errors and abstentions are reported separately.
