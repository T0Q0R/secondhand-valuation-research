Let kη = |{i : ∆i > 2η}|. Equation (1) implies T ∗(η, B) = 0 if
and only if B ≥kη: each strictly positive residual gap needs unit query probability. In particular,

The lower bound follows because some marginal is at most B/n; equal marginals attain it. Thus
honest precision, query capacity and strategic gain cannot be specified independently in this alias
family.
This is compatible with existing truthful predictors. Natural-versus-strategic missingness ambi-
guity is explicit in [5], whose Theorems 1 and 2 give minimum-cut optimality and a MAX-ensemble
characterization for binary classification. A predictor can eliminate withholding gains by chang-
ing its predictions, while violating the uniform error requirement imposed here. Our lower bound
restricts that escape route; it does not contradict truthful classification or establish impossibility
under average MAE alone. History-dependent quote rules satisfy this frontier only when they also
meet its verification-neutrality and uniform-precision assumptions on the specified family. An arbi-
trary history cap cannot be placed inside that mechanism class without checking these conditions.
A two-mode constructed example at B = 1, η = 0 yields 72/17 for the public oracle, 72/13 for
the best global mixture of eight fixture rules, and 8 for risk ordering. The eight rules are the six
priority permutations of three items, risk ordering by residual leverage (∆i −2η)+, and no query;
this fixture library differs from the twelve-rule empirical CDM library in Section 3.2. These are
artificial linear quote units, not empirical log-price effects. All 32 budget/precision cases passed a
separate auditor.
What is tested in the learned-model experiment.
Equation (1) applies to the single-
recoverable alias family, verification-neutral quotes and a uniform error constraint. The empirical
model has four fields, can hide two and is evaluated by average MAE, so these assumptions do not
establish its empirical optimum or predict a feedback sign. We use the frontier as a scope boundary.
The recovery analysis instead gives a finite diagnostic on the actual model class: enumerate legal
partial recoveries and test whether a query raises the quote above both its pre-query and honest
anchors. Section 7.1 performs that check.
