A returned profile with small remaining regret is a local deviation diagnostic, not a certificate

3.2
Predictors, public scores and comparator identity
Suppression-aware valuation (SAV) combines an augmented price predictor, a suppression acqui-
sition head and simulated query-feedback training. The original static-robust comparator changes
the prediction training objective without that feedback procedure.
Counterfactual deviation-
constrained mixture verification (CDM) instead fixes the price model and calibrates probabili-
ties over complete budget-feasible query rules.
Its calibration objective constrains stored own-
report/peer-profile rows, not all unseen types. The original library contains twelve rules and five
calibration peer-profile families; expanding the finite library does not itself establish out-of-sample
incentive compatibility. These original candidates provide the context for the matched experiment,
whose newly trained initial-objective factor must not be renamed SAV or static robust.
For training targets, let qs be a cross-fitted quote under report state s, qs\f the quote after
recovering field f, qH the honest quote, and y the log advertised price. The three target quantities
are

A common multioutput ExtraTrees head learns these quantities from encoded public inputs [14].
Honest records and targets construct training labels only; deployment does not directly substitute
them into the scores. Each policy chooses its highest-scoring publicly eligible field and queries
only positive scores. Top-B ranks these per-listing scores, using the declared stable tie rule. The
score-minimax control instead balances positive residual scores through query marginals. It solves
a score surrogate, not the true unilateral incentive problem.
3.3
Evaluation quantities
For one returned report, let qH be its model’s honest log quote, y the log-transformed advertised
price, q0 the no-query quote, q1 the quote conditional on the selected query, and p its marginal
query probability. Here y = log(1 + price) and µ = (1 −p)q0 + pq1. The incentive coordinate is
[µ −qH]+; it compares the model with itself, rather than with the advertised target. The separate
realized-positive quantity is (1−p)[q0−qH]++p[q1−qH]+. Honest price error is | exp(qH)−exp(y)|,
and attacked price error is
(1 −p)| exp(q0) −exp(y)| + p| exp(q1) −exp(y)|.
The subtraction of one cancels inside each absolute difference. Taking an absolute error after aver-
aging the quotes would define a different metric. Failed-query probability is p times the indicator
that the selected field cannot be recovered; such an attempt still consumes capacity. Signed log
bias is µ −y, while expected squared log error averages the two squared endpoint errors before
aggregation.
