descriptive outcomes, with honest MAE as the precision coordinate. The joint outcome vector,
continuous paired effects and Pareto trade-offs take priority over any single ranking. This reporting
choice is retrospective; it is not a preregistered change in the original experiment.
For the unilateral audit, hold peers’ returned reports fixed and enumerate all feasible reports
of one seller, reallocating the whole batch for each. Writing u(r) for that seller’s expected log
quote minus concealment cost, the deviation envelope is [maxr u(r)−u(rhonest)]+; remaining regret
instead subtracts the utility of the returned report. Thus a small regret does not establish a small
gain over honesty. Main empirical concealment costs are zero. All quantities are aggregated over
the retained listings; nonconverged responses remain in the analysis. The finite oracle’s constructed
quote units must not be interpreted as empirical log-price effects.
