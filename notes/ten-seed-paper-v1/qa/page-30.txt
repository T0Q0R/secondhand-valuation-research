Conditional probability-stability diagnostics
Takeaway. Randomized allocation admits a conditional sensitivity bound, but small field margins
and scores near zero limit its applicability. Stability alone does not imply lower strategic inflation.
Fix the positive-score support I, the queried field of each item, and budget B. For two score
vectors g, g′ with all supported scores at least m > 0, let δ = ∥g −g′∥∞. Define the score-minimax

For any fixed feasible allocation, the two objectives differ by at most δ, which proves the value
bound after minimization. The function h(t, x) = [1 −t/x]+ is separately 1/m-Lipschitz in t and x
for t ≥0, x ≥m: on its positive branch, t/x2 ≤1/x, and it is continuous at the branch boundary.
Applying the value bound proves the marginal bound.
The active allocation set may change;

If both pre-query and recovered quotes change by at most ϵ, and the primed recovery span is at
most R, expected quotes differ by at most ϵ + R min{1, 2δ/m}. If these conditions hold uniformly
over all own reports at fixed peer reports, costs are unchanged, and truthful quotes are verification-
neutral, the positive unilateral-gain envelope changes by at most 2ϵ + R min{1, 2δ/m}. This is
not a guarantee for adapted peer responses, equilibrium selection, training convergence, or a fixed
realization of the sampling randomness.
Three examples delimit the claim. With two items and B = 1, swapping scores (1 + δ, 1) and
(1, 1+δ) changes the first item’s top-B probability by one, but its minimax probability by δ/(2+δ).
In contrast, swapping (2δ, δ) and (δ, 2δ) changes its minimax probability by 1/3 even as δ tends
to zero. Finally, one fully queried item with two fields whose scores swap as in the first example
can have a unit quote jump if the two recovery quotes are zero and one. The score lower bound
and fixed-field condition therefore cannot simply be removed. An exact-arithmetic checker covers
2,916 score-pair/budget cases, including 510 active-set changes; 108 production/linear-program
comparisons agree to floating-point precision. These finite checks supplement the proof.
