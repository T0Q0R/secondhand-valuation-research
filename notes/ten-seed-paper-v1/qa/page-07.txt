Signed mechanism decomposition
Takeaway. Freezing truthful queries cannot separate recovery from capacity in this replay model;

For each fixed peer profile and evaluated mixture, retain its saved maximizing own report s∗.
Let qH be the truthful quote, qh the unqueried reported quote, qL the quote after the same rule’s
highest-scoring eligible query with capacity relaxed, and qA the hard-budget quote. Ineligible or
nonpositive scores still cause no query in the relaxed reference. Then
E = qh −qH,
R = E[qL] −qh,
C = E[qA] −E[qL],
G = E + R + C.
All components are signed and evaluated at the same s∗. Mixture expectation precedes report
maximization. The relaxed query is a diagnostic, not a feasible deployment policy. The original
truthful-query anchor would give zero recovery: that query targets a naturally absent field, which
remains absent when another field is withheld. Its remaining term would conflate recovery and
