Ten-seed recovery-path diagnostics
Figure 13 retains every fitting seed and all four nonzero path/gap comparisons at primary capacity.
Small weights can reverse individual seed effects; their means do not imply per-seed monotonic
improvement.
All zero-weight controls remain in the public source tables, although their self-
difference is identically zero and is not colored as new evidence.
The original three-seed recovery-edge measurement, separate from the ten-seed performance
expansion, gave mean positive gain increments of 0.001790 to 0.000693 (synthetic), 0.001648 to
0.001048 (JUCars), and 0.000788 to 0.000450 (MUCars) at λ = 1, β = 0.5. They remained positive,
so the zero-edge-penalty premise of Proposition 3 was not achieved. The pilot is not a ten-seed
mechanism remeasurement or proof of a unique causal explanation.
The new four-stage ACO audit initially stopped because its independent distance reconstruction
subtracted archived float32 bank/query arrays, whereas production promoted both to float64 before
distance calculations. The corrected, separately versioned auditor matches that arithmetic contract
and passed the full 144-cell chain. The failed receipt, original checker and new source closure remain
public; no tolerance, production model or saved result was changed to obtain a pass.
