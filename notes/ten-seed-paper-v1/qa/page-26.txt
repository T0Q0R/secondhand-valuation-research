External execution and numerical reproducibility.
An independently operated Mac com-
pleted the six-cell synthetic/seed-13 training-and-evaluation package. Training/evaluation popula-
tions and tree backbones matched, but residual-network parameters and aggregate outcomes did
not meet the original numerical tolerances. Those failures are retained. Subsequent saved-model
diagnostics reproduced each machine’s own parameters and found the first recorded cross-machine
difference in first-batch output weight gradients. Hidden intermediate activations were not cap-
tured, so a specific arithmetic primitive or environment was not causally identified. Effect directions
at the three tested budgets agreed in this one seed, but that is not numerical equivalence or a multi-
seed external confirmation. An isolated float64 engineering candidate has not replaced the reported
float32 experiments and has not established cross-machine performance.
