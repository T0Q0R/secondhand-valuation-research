H.1
Recent-method identity and training boundaries
The adapters use the same fixed price backends as ACO-1, not each paper’s original predictive task.
SEFA, GDFS, DIME and explanation-ranked AFA learn acquisition using four training-quantile
price classes; their scores feed a shared hard-capacity, one-query allocator. This does not make
them native strategic regressors. Nonquery context is available, natural absence is unrecoverable,
and deployment scores do not observe a hidden value or its missingness cause. Hidden-mileage
perturbation checks left the corresponding public-state scores unchanged. Predictive information
scores and policy probabilities are used as cross-listing priority proxies without a theorem of cross-
listing cardinal comparability.
SEFA used its oﬀicial MIT implementation, commit 3af8da2, configurations 1/5/9, 120 epochs
per trial and their original latent-sampling settings. GDFS and DIME used the comparison im-
plementations in that repository, retaining their relaxed-one-hot/temperature and entropy-scaled
value/epsilon-greedy cores. Their configurations 1/5/9 use 80 predictor-pretraining epochs and 15
epochs per annealing stage. The task adapter starts from legal public reports and trains at most
two recoveries, uses Adam and stagewise best validation states, and does not reproduce the orig-
inal learning-rate scheduler. This is a sourced task adaptation, not an unchanged original-author
reproduction.
Explanation-ranked AFA used the oﬀicial repository commit d08f39a, its tabular MLP and
three-layer, four-head GPT, hidden width 128 and embedding width 64. A fitting-partition 250-
tree CatBoost classifier supplies TreeSHAP rankings; field-value and missing-indicator absolute
contributions for the training label are summed. Natural missingness and numeric category coding
are task adaptations. The stages comprise 250 masked-predictor, 200 ranking-supervised and 16
policy-trajectory epochs, with at most two recoveries per training trajectory and a fixed initial
action marker at history-free deployment. Learning rates 0.001, 0.0005, 0.0003 form a fixed local
grid; the last is an additional sensitivity value rather than an original default.
All four classification adapters select among three configurations using the same training-only
80:20 split and then refit on all training records.
SEFA uses its masked validation NLL and
selected epoch, while the other three use mean pre/post-query NLL and their full fixed schedule.
Thus selection effort is documented but their validation objectives are not identical. No evaluation
outcome is used to select a configuration. ACO-1 instead retains the declared training-bank rule
and fixed neighbor sensitivities described in Appendix E. These differences, the fixed 1,000-record
completion cohort, and three fitting seeds bound cross-family conclusions.
