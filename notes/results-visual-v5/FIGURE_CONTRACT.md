# Results figure contract

Four new quantitative figures supplement two existing paired-effect forests. Matplotlib/Python is the established project backend. No training, response search, new CI or model superiority claim is added. Existing frozen scientific sources stay unchanged. These are experimental-results figures, not AI-generated illustrations. Styling is newly implemented; only the existing self-contained PDF-to-LaTeX vector adapter is structurally adapted.

1. Cap trade-offs. Question: does inflation reduction survive predictive-error and failed-query costs? Two equal panels show the same 12 robust-estimator primary source/rule means at 10% capacity, first attacked MAE change%, then failed-query change pp, against inflation reduction%. All three fitted seeds are aggregated equally, all three sources/four rules retained; markers encode rule and source color. No confidence inference from means. Data: corroborated full response summary, filter robust/.1 and paired original/capped. No currency mixing: error uses within-source relative change. Positive x is favorable; positive y is unfavorable. Main §7.5, beside the joint-cost argument.
2. Cost mechanism. Question: what accompanies the local feedback reversal? Panel a shows saved-state zero-cost hidden counts and regret-based positive-cost identification bounds for both feedback settings, all four scenarios; panel b shows each of three paired fitting-seed inflation improvements (no feedback minus feedback), with mean horizontal strokes. Bounds are not confidence intervals; no point estimate is invented inside them. No interpolation across heterogeneous costs; all nonconverged batches retained. Main §7.7. Counts/cost inference is synthetic robust/suppression/top-B only, matching the stated sensitivity.
3. Conditional uncertainty. Question: why did full MUCars evaluation leave seed uncertainty dominant? One stacked-bar axis displays seed/batch/interaction variances in absolute units for both masking/suppression allocators under capped and uncapped designs. Percent seed labels accompany absolute components; the cohort and weights change, so this is not a pure replication-size comparison. All four pertinent designs retained; full 48-row decomposition remains evidence. Main §7.8. No new training or CI.
4. Final-record boundary. Question: where does the restricted envelope cost occur? Panel a exposes covered complete, covered naturally incomplete and abstained counts for both real sources among the original1000 records. Panel b shows all three MUCars fitting seeds' honest-MAE change on shared covered support versus the missing-category subset. JU/MU currencies are never pooled; second panel explicitly MUCars currency. Exact singleton zero-cost and concentration are structural within this restricted domain, not universal mechanism novelty. AppendixG.3; brief main§5.3 pointer. Mileage observed/fixed, all abstentions and all seeds shown.
