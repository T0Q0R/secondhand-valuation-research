6.2
Evaluation, uncertainty and audit scope
Fixed models are evaluated with four starts and at most twenty response sweeps; quotes/errors
are integrated analytically over query marginals and every legal unilateral deviation is checked
with fresh reallocation. The original study has 4,320 cells over thirty source–seed pairs. Its 58
conditional contrasts per source/capacity produce 174 primary contrasts. Paired crossed seed–batch
resampling preserves method pairing [15]: the original analysis uses 1,000 resamples, whereas the
core extensions use 4,000, including exploratory studentized maximum-deviation bands [16] over
216 RPL metric contrasts. Fixed cohorts, ten clusters and fitting-only randomness prevent a finite-
sample family-wise or new-training-population guarantee. Interactions are differences of feedback
effects between allocators; a positive interaction alone does not make feedback beneficial.
The three-seed completion study has 1,719 cells including references. RPL then reuses its 162
pilot cells and adds 378 to retain ten seeds, six configurations and three capacities (540 cells).
Parameter and availability scans remain three-seed. Fit/outcome/analysis/statistics-check phases
cover the original 4,320 cells and 1,206 distinct claim-bearing extension cells, including all 540 RPL
cells (Table 2). Stage counts are 162 pilot RPL, 90 search, 432 SEFA/GDFS/DIME/explanation,
144 ACO and 378 additional RPL cells. The 540-cell RPL audit overlaps the pilot; remaining
support cells are not upgraded.
The checks reconstruct fitting roles, recorded outcomes, legal deviations and saved statistics
but share prediction/loading primitives; they are not an independent full-stack implementation.
An additional bit-mask oracle checks 1,188 fixture and 521,164 real-record field transitions without
sharing state/query logic. Historical failures remain recorded, including the corrected ACO audi-
tor’s float32-versus-float64 distance mismatch. Human scientific approval and external numerical
reproduction are separate; the latter failed its original tolerances. Appendix E indexes the complete
protocols, failure receipts and audit limits.
