Full removals, default scans and seed heterogeneity
The original component experiment toggles suppression scoring (S), feedback (F) and gap loss (G).
All eight configurations retain backbone, augmentation, error and anchoring terms, with equal role
separation and epoch budgets. Thus removal of two or all three components is evaluated against
a trained baseline (Figure 9). These switches do not constitute three independently established
innovations.
Figure 10 retains all ten fitting seeds and four nonzero path/gap comparisons, each with its
same-gap zero-path reference. Negative seed effects at smaller weights remain. A separate original
three-seed recovery-edge pilot found mean positive increments decreasing from .001790 to .000693
(synthetic), .001648 to .001048 (JUCars) and .000788 to .000450 (MUCars) at λ = 1, β = .5.
All remained positive: zero path loss was not achieved, and the pilot is not relabeled as ten-seed
evidence.
Core scans vary gap weight 0, .1, .25, .5, 1, 2, tolerance 0, .01, .02, .05, .1 and feedback rounds
1, 2, 4, at 10/30% budgets. Implementation scans vary anchoring, attack weight, learning rate,
epochs, head trees and width. Figures 11 and 12 explicitly mark every scanned value and engineering
default; matching default observations are reused exactly, not retrained or selected on evaluation
outcomes. These three-seed scans do not retune ten-seed RPL or prove an optimum. The separate
availability stress adds 0/10/30% natural absence to fixed models (189 cells, 63 reused), with seven
policies and fresh response search but no new fitting; it is a controlled perturbation rather than a
new market or actual-seller validation.

Historical controls and reproducibility index
The public repository preserves the full pre-compression manuscript at commit f5da885, including
the original 17 figures and 18 tables. Current figure source data, all fitted comparisons and failed
receipts remain available.
This compact appendix summarizes secondary controls rather than
duplicating their full tables; no experiment, negative outcome or nonconverged row was excluded.
Original comparisons.
In ten-seed original-protocol comparisons at 10% capacity, comparator-
minus-SAV inflation differences for static robust are −.009091 (synthetic), −.002906 (JUCars) and
−.000829 (MUCars). The synthetic interval excludes zero; both real-source intervals cross it. These
620 cells/source are not the matched factorial. In separately recomputed common-response controls,
ordinary benefit and ACO beat SAV on synthetic/JUCars; some larger-cap MUCars comparisons
favor SAV. No evaluation-best tuning cap replaces the declared role. CDM’s finite rows/library
and adapted support-margin fallbacks also preclude a universal original-method advantage.
