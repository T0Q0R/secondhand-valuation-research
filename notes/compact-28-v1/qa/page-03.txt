The platform may query at most one publicly missing field per listing and at most B = ⌊bn⌋
fields on every draw. A concealed recorded value is recovered; natural absence remains unavailable.
Both attempts spend capacity. Deployment scores use public records and model outputs, not latent
recoverability or advertised targets. Seller utility is expected log quote minus concealment cost,
zero in the main analysis. Response search reallocates the whole batch for each candidate own
report with peers fixed.
SAV combines an augmented predictor, suppression scoring and query-feedback training. Static
robust changes the prediction objective without that feedback. CDM fixes the price model and
calibrates a mixture of twelve feasible rules on stored report/peer rows from five profile families; it
has no unseen-type guarantee. The matched factorial below trains new initial-objective fits, rather
than renaming these original checkpoints.

3.2
Joint outcomes and finite responses
Write y = log(1+price), let q0, q1 be pre-query and selected-query quotes and p the query marginal.
With µ = (1 −p)q0 + pq1, inflation is [µ −qH]+. It differs from the realized-positive quantity
(1 −p)[q0 −qH]+ + p[q1 −qH]+. Honest and attacked MAE are respectively
|eqH −ey|,
(1 −p)|eq0 −ey| + p|eq1 −ey|.
Averaging quotes before absolute error would define another metric. Failed-query probability is p
times the indicator of unavailable recovery, aggregated per all listings; failure still spends capacity.
Signed log bias is µ−y. Inflation, attacked MAE and failed queries are co-primary descriptive out-
comes, with honest MAE measuring precision. This retrospective choice guards against a constant
predictor, which has zero inflation regardless of pricing error; it is not a preregistered endpoint
change.
The unilateral deviation envelope is [maxr u(r) −u(rhonest)]+ with fixed peers and fresh batch
allocation. Remaining regret subtracts u(rreturned) instead. Small regret does not imply small gain
over honesty or a globally selected market equilibrium. All returned profiles, including nonconverged
ones, remain in aggregation.
