Final-record envelope.
Three robust/no-feedback fits per real source enumerate categorical
transmission/fuel/condition completions from training vocabularies, with mileage observed and
fixed. Unsupported categories or missing mileage abstain. Current records are included; singleton
domains leave quotes unchanged. Of 999 covered MUCars records, 971 have singleton completions
and zero cost; the 28 naturally incomplete records concentrate its error increase. Of 801 covered
JUCars records, 800 are singletons and all 801 retain the same quote.

Recent-method adaptations and numerical comparison
All five acquisition families use the same frozen price backends, one-query hard-capacity inter-
face, public nonquery context and unrecoverable natural absence. The four classification adapters
train on four price-quantile classes and use acquisition scores as cross-listing priorities; no cardinal
comparability theorem is asserted. Hidden-mileage perturbations leave their public-state scores
unchanged. The shared design has three sources, three seeds, two backends and six budgets (540
cells), plus 36 ACO sensitivities. Table 6 gives every primary family mean.
