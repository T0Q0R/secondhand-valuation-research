7.3
Five task-adapted recent acquisition families on fixed price models
ACO is a clean-room one-query adaptation. SEFA uses oﬀicial model code inside a changed task
interface; GDFS and DIME use the SEFA repository’s comparison implementations; explanation-
ranked AFA uses oﬀicial network classes with adapted ranking generation and training. Origi-
nal code provenance therefore does not make the complete experiment an oﬀicial reproduction.
The classification-to-regression, observation, selection and budget differences are specified in Ap-
pendix C. No claim of reproducing or defeating the original papers follows from this comparison.
Each of the five adapters covered three sources, three fitting seeds, two frozen price backends and
six capacities: 540 common-grid cells. ACO additionally retained 36 fixed-neighbor sensitivities.
Holding source, seed, backend and capacity fixed, honest MAE agreed across methods to less than
10−6, as required when only acquisition changes. ACO’s declared k = 25 was used throughout; no
evaluation-best neighbor count replaced it. Figure 6 shows every family’s budget curve alongside
available matched risk/benefit controls. At 10% capacity with the robust backend, GDFS had the
lowest family-mean inflation on JUCars (0.01013), whereas SEFA was lowest on MUCars (0.00660)
and synthetic (0.01067). No recent family minimized inflation on all three sources. Simple risk
or benefit remained competitive or better in several mask-aware comparisons.
