Recovery diagnosis and recovery-path learning
5.1
Separating recovery from competition
For fixed peers and an evaluated mixture, retain its maximizing own report s∗. Let qh be the
unqueried report quote, qL the quote after the same rule’s highest positive eligible query with
capacity relaxed, and qA the hard-budget quote. At the same report,
E = qh −qH,
R = E[qL] −qh,
C = E[qA] −E[qL],
G = E + R + C.
All terms are signed; mixture expectation precedes report maximization. The relaxed query is
diagnostic. Reusing the truthful query would target a naturally missing field and confound recovery
with competition. In the 10/12/14 example, E = R = 2, C = −2(1 −p) and G = 2 +2p: successful
partial recovery can increase the gain of a nonmonotone predictor.
