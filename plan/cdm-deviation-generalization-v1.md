# CDM common-profile deviation generalization, exploratory v1

Frozen before inspecting the new tail summaries. This is an explicitly
post-hoc diagnostic motivated by the completed CDM study's unfavorable mean
inflation results. It does not replace the original primary outcome, retune
probabilities, train new models or establish prospective confirmation.

## Immutable scope

Use the complete independently audited nine-pair CDM fixed-estimator union:
three sources, three fitting seeds, both saved price estimators and all six
original capacities. Preserve all twelve deterministic rules, uniform mixture
and the training-calibrated CDM mixture: 108 source/seed/price/budget cases,
1,512 method cells. Preserve the primary capacity 0.1 and disclose every other
capacity as exploratory. Only existing recorded-value replay is available.
Use no validation target in constructing peer profiles or scores.

## Common held-out peer-profile bank

For each saved evaluation batch, construct the same five profile types as
calibration: truthful reports; no-query maximum legal transformed quote;
legal random reports with generator 603 reset per batch; the saved suppression
response; the saved benefit response. The latter two were generated using the
original four-start/twenty-sweep search. Bind their outcome files and report
states. All fourteen methods are measured against the identical five peer
profiles, rather than each method's own returned peers. These five profiles
are not all possible peer-report combinations and some depend on the saved
price/score models. No new equilibrium or joint-response optimum is claimed.

For each listing, profile and legal unilateral report, recalculate its quote
under all twelve rules. Its truthful comparison is recomputed against the
same peers with a newly allocated batch. The deviation row is the signed
log-quote difference minus the original concealment cost. For mixture alpha,
take the expectation first and then maximize over legal reports and truncate
at zero. Truthful reporting remains a legal zero-gain option.

## Measures fixed before opening the new summaries

For each method define e(i,p)=max(0,max_h D(i,h,p) alpha). Every listing has
equal weight within each of the five profiles, irrespective of the number
of legal reports. Save these entries, their maximizing report and complete
signed matrices with locators. Report whole-case mean, maximum, empirical
95th percentile and exact top-5%-mass mean (CVaR95), plus corresponding
per-batch metrics. Fractional boundary mass is used when 5% of the entry
count is not an integer. Prices/utility remain log1p, not expected currency.

Compare CDM with the predeclared risk rule, benefit rule, uniform mixture,
and calibration-selected pure rule. Retain all other deterministic results.
Never select a reference from the evaluation tails. Whole-case maxima and
quantiles are descriptive summaries across fitting seeds. Conditional paired
intervals, if supplied, use differences in batch-level measures, average
over the fitted seeds, then resample the ten shared batches. They do not
certify maxima over new markets, training populations or unobserved peers.

## Verification and refusal conditions

Bind completed source/audit generations, raw prediction/score arrays, cohort
IDs, model/head bindings, saved response inputs, calibration probabilities,
plan and analysis sources. Check these bytes remain unchanged at completion.
Require capacity-feasible candidate reallocation including failed queries.
Independently reconstruct saved public scores from the frozen model/head;
use the sorted-peer/bisect oracle, separate from the vectorized Market oracle,
to verify every new matrix and every method/metric at every capacity.
Software tests compare small-market direct full-batch allocations with both
oracles, including ties, natural missingness, zero/full capacity and
expectation-before-max behavior. Smoke output is never source evidence.

If CDM's held-out maximum/tail is worse than simple controls, retain the
failure. Even favorable held-out tails would establish only this common-bank
retrospective diagnostic; finite calibration optimality does not imply
universal incentive compatibility or restore the original mean-outcome claim.
