## Mechanism decomposition

**Correction from implementation,2026-10-07:** the truthful-allocation anchor
below is algebraically valid but its replay term is identically zero for the
current itemwise estimator and natural-missing query semantics. It therefore
cannot isolate recovery from capacity. The executed diagnostic instead uses
the same public rule's capacity-relaxed query as an anchor, as specified in
`mechanism-decomposition-v1.md`. Retain the original proposal below to make
the correction explicit; do not present its competition term as pure capacity.

For each fixed seller deviationh, with peers fixed, letq(r,a) denote the
seller's quote after environment replay. Let a0 and ah be allocations under
truthful and deviated public reports; both are recomputed on the whole batch.
The exact signed difference can be decomposed into:

    estimation = q(rh,none) − q(r0,none)
    replay = [q(rh,a0)−q(rh,none)] − [q(r0,a0)−q(r0,none)]
    competition = q(rh,ah) − q(rh,a0)
    total = estimation + replay + competition.

The frozen-a0 term is a diagnostic intervention, not a deployable comparator.
Specify query handling whena0 names a now-observed field; it leaves the value
unchanged and cannot manufacture recovery. Decompose at the same maximizing
deviationh*, and only then clip total gain. Maximizing or clipping each
component separately destroys the identity. Record failure counts and
capacity displacement explicitly. A worst gain over a finite attack search
is not observed strategic seller behavior.

Mechanism hypotheses: natural-missing proportion, optional-field price
association, withholding opportunities, failed replay, ranking displacement
and attack-response models. MUCars's zero-query difference is an estimator
diagnostic; it is not proof that the entire gain comes from the estimator.
Evaluate alternate response models using the same saved/report grids and
retain all failures. Do not choose one explanatory feature after source
results and describe it as confirmed causation.

## Statistical unit and uncertainty

Ten seeds quantify initialization/procedural variation. Repeatedly fitting
the same training records does not by itself quantify training-population
uncertainty. A nested training+batch bootstrap requires outer resampling of
training records and full refitting of every estimator/head/feedback loop,
then resampling held-out batches inside each fitted replicate. Without those
refits, label the intervals seed+evaluation-batch uncertainty only.

Use paired source×capacity×method interactions with seed and shared batch
effects; check distributional assumptions before selecting a mixed-effects
model. A hierarchical Bayesian analysis needs explicit likelihood, priors,
posterior diagnostics and estimand. Three sources alone cannot support a
population-wide random-source claim. Retain unadjusted exploratory intervals
as such and plan multiplicity handling for confirmatory comparisons.
