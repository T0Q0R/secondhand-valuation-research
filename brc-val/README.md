Design: `../notes/design/创新候选-BRC-Val.md`. This is an independent research
candidate, not a new result of SAV/CDM/EMC and not a deployment contract.

The separate real-source feasibility screen now fits three train-only additive
price models and independently replays3000 envelopes/180 batch-budget cells.
The primary gate fails on all three sources: none of their10 batches can
meet the current midpoint-anchor reserve at10% field-query allowance, charging
four fields per complete audit. See `../notes/brc-source-feasibility-results-2026-10-07-v1.md`.
These are conservative declared-domain budget screens, not a physical-market
impossibility theorem or a matched performance comparison. No full performance
matrix or practical success is claimed for this version.

Current successful generation: `outputs/finite-demo-02`. The independently
reconstructed rational audit checks all729 public profiles and4096 true-type
and legal-report cases, hard audit cardinality, bounded quotes, exact expected
prices and the probability allocation's optimality conditions. There is no
fresh model fit or real-data evaluation. Audits recover a full2-field bundle;
they are not comparable to a single-field query without cost matching.

Generation01's independent audit found a floating-point cardinality failure;
its original source snapshots, results and failure receipt remain immutable.
Its original hard-budget certification is withdrawn. Generation02 uses exact
rational interval boundaries and an exact represented-probability sum check.
Positive disclosure cost, audit failure, post-draw withdrawal and risk-seeking
utility require different models; method novelty remains unverified.
